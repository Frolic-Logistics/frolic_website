Consent to identity verification

Version
2026-09-01
Status
Draft, not in force
Applies to
Travellers

Why we ask

To post trips on Frolic, you verify who you are, that you hold a driving licence, and that your vehicle is registered. This helps keep senders and their parcels safe.

Senders do not need to verify their identity.

What you agree to

By accepting this consent, you agree that Frolic and its verification provider may check the following:

  • Aadhaar: you sign in to DigiLocker on our verification provider’s page and choose to share your Aadhaar details with them.
  • Driving licence: you give your licence number and date of birth, and our provider checks them against the government’s Parivahan records.
  • Vehicle registration: you give your vehicle’s registration number, and our provider checks its registration certificate.

Who does the checking

[Name of the verification provider], a licensed verification provider acting on Frolic’s instructions.

What Frolic keeps

  • whether each check succeeded, and when
  • your name as shown on your Aadhaar
  • the last four digits of your Aadhaar
  • the vehicle classes on your driving licence and how long it is valid
  • your vehicle’s registration number in masked form (state, RTO and last four digits) and how long its registration is valid
  • the provider’s reference for each check

What Frolic never stores

Your Aadhaar number, Aadhaar XML, driving licence number, date of birth, the address or photo on an identity document, any image of an identity document, or the name of your vehicle’s registered owner.

Withdrawing your consent

You can withdraw this consent at any time [how: in the app’s settings, or by email]. You will then not be able to post new trips. Withdrawing does not undo checks already done.

How long we keep it

[Retention period for verification results, including after you delete your account, to be confirmed.]
